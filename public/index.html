    About INTO THE VOID

    ITV is a community music platform where DJs rotate through shared playlists and listeners vote, earn XP, and level up — all in one live room.

    • Guests can chat and listen without an account.
    • Registered users manage playlists and join the DJ queue.
    • One account, one active tab — the stage keeps playing while you browse panels here.
